clara Invoicing over chat for Spanish freelancers.

Freelancers issue invoices over WhatsApp or Telegram, by text or voice. Clara logs expenses from a photo of the receipt, shows the quarter at a glance (Form 303 and 130) and exports the books to the accountant. Invoices are registered with Verifactu.

hypothekas Mortgage comparator for Spain.

Compares mortgages from the main Spanish banks. It works out the monthly payment with the current Euribor for fixed, variable and mixed rates, adds the purchase costs and taxes of each province, and shows the housing aid programs available. It is free and you don't need an account.

commandbench Deck analyzer for Magic: The Gathering.

Magic: The Gathering is a card game where every player builds their own deck. You paste a deck list or a link, and CommandBench tells you how strong the deck is, what strategy it plays, which card combos it has and which cards you could swap. The suggestions come from what other players put in their public decks.

chest-ct-scan Lung disease classification on chest CT.

A fine-tuned ResNet-50 that sorts chest CT scans into four classes: adenocarcinoma, large cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma and normal. The images get CLAHE histogram equalization and heavy augmentation, and training uses Adam with ReduceLROnPlateau.
