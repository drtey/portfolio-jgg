Predictive analysis in liver transplantation for hepatocarcinoma: circadian timing impact

Volunteer work at University Hospital Virgen de la Arrixaca (Murcia, Spain). I conduct predictive analysis examining how time-of-day impacts outcomes in liver transplants for hepatocarcinoma patients, based on chronobiological principles. Using Kaplan-Meier survival models, Cox regression, and machine learning algorithms.
